Welcome to the Chemistry Laboratory

The Chemistry Laboratory at Abeda Inamdar Senior College provides students with an environment for practical learning, experimentation and scientific study.

This Chemistry Lab Management System is designed to help organize laboratory equipment, chemicals, experiments and laboratory records in a simple and efficient manner.

About Our Project

During our field visit to the Chemistry Lab, we observed the importance of proper management of laboratory equipment, chemicals, records and safety procedures.

Our Main Features

  • Equipment Management
  • Chemical Stock Management
  • Experiment Records
  • Laboratory Safety
  • Student and Teacher Activities
  • Lab Assistant Management
